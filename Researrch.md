### AI-Based Recyclability Assessment of Damaged Packaging Using Computer Vision

**Problem Statement:**

Identifying whether packaging is recyclable becomes difficult when recycling labels are faded, damaged, or partially obscured, or when packaging consists of multiple materials. Incorrect classification can lead to improper waste segregation and contamination of recyclable waste streams.

The objective of this project is to develop an AI-based computer vision system that analyzes images of damaged packaging, identifies visible material characteristics, detects damaged or unreadable recycling symbols, and assesses whether the packaging is recyclable according to predefined local recycling guidelines. The system will provide an explainable recycling recommendation and flag uncertain cases for manual inspection when the available visual information is insufficient.
